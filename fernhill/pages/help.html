How grading works

Courses and lessons

  • Search ignores accents, so cafe finds Café. Course length is the total of its lessons' times.
  • Some courses need another course completed first (every lesson done). Full courses can't take more students.
  • Your progress is the share of lessons you've marked complete. Lessons are taken in order; Next marks the lesson complete and goes to the following one, across modules.
  • Notes save automatically as you type.

Quizzes

  • Each quiz has a time limit and 2 attempts. Your best attempt counts.
  • "Select all that apply" questions need every right option and no wrong one.
  • Number answers accept any way of writing the number (3, 3.0). Text answers ignore capitals and spaces around them.
  • Percentages are rounded to the nearest whole number.

Assignments

  • Upload a PDF, TXT, DOCX or PY file of up to 2 MB, or write your answer. Some assignments need a minimum number of words.
  • Late work loses 10% of its score for each day late, up to 50%.
  • You can resubmit; your grade stays until your instructor regrades it.

Your grade

  • Quizzes are 40% of the grade and assignments 60%. Your lowest quiz score is dropped.
  • Work that hasn't been graded yet doesn't count. Missing work past its due date counts as 0.
  • Instructors grade out of each assignment's points (halves such as 9.5 are fine, more than the points isn't), and release grades when every submission is graded.

Certificates

  • Available once every lesson is complete and your grade is 70% or more. It shows the date you finished.

Discussion

  • Posts and replies can be up to 1,000 characters. You can like a post once.
